UNIT 02

Electrical Quantities & Ohm's Law

study the basic electrical quantities used in electrical work, including voltage, current, resistance, and power.

Ohm's Law explains the relationship between voltage, current, and resistance and is one of the most important calculations used by electricians.

KEY CONCEPTS

Voltage

Voltage is the electrical pressure that pushes current through a circuit. Voltage is measured in volts (V).

Current

Current is the flow of electrical charge through a circuit. Current is measured in amperes (A).

Resistance

Resistance opposes the flow of current. Resistance is measured in ohms (Ω).

Power

Electrical power is the rate at which electrical energy is used. Power is measured in watts (W).

OHM'S LAW
Voltage, Current & Resistance

Ohm's Law can be used to calculate voltage, current, or resistance when the other two values are known.

V = I × R

Where:

V = Voltage
I = Current
R = Resistance
